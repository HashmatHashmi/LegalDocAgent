MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is entered into as of March 3, 2026 between Alpha Technologies Ltd. (the "Company") and Beta Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (the "Recipient"), each a "Party".

1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either Party to the other, in any form, that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

2. Obligations
Each Party shall protect the other Party's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own, and shall use it only to evaluate a potential business relationship between the Parties.

3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that is publicly available, independently developed, or lawfully received from a third party without restriction.

4. Term
This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the date above. Each Party's confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination.

5. Termination
Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days written notice to the other Party.

6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan. Any dispute shall be resolved by the courts of Peshawar.

7. Notices
All notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses stated above.

8. Entire Agreement
This is the entire agreement of the Parties on its subject and may be amended only in writing signed by both Parties.

Signed by the authorised representatives of each Party.
