EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement is made on 15 June 2026 between Zenith Software (Pvt) Ltd. (the "Company") and Mr. Bilal Ahmed (the "Employee").

1. Position and Duties
The Employee is appointed as Software Engineer and shall perform any other duties as required by the Company. Working hours are 9am to 6pm; the Employee shall work additional hours when required without additional compensation.

2. Salary
The Employee shall receive a monthly salary of Rs. 120,000. The Company may withhold or deduct any amount from salary at its discretion.

3. Probation and Termination
The Company may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice or cause. The Employee must give three (3) months written notice of resignation.

4. Service Bond
The Employee shall serve the Company for a minimum period of three years. If the Employee resigns earlier, the Employee shall pay Rs. 500,000 as liquidated damages and reimburse all training costs.

5. Non-Compete
For five (5) years after leaving the Company, the Employee shall not work for any competitor anywhere in Pakistan or engage in any similar business.

6. Intellectual Property
The Employee assigns to the Company all inventions, work product and intellectual property created during employment, whether or not related to the Company's business.

7. Confidentiality
The Employee shall keep all Company information confidential in perpetuity.

8. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Pakistan.

Signed by both parties.
