RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 1st January 2026 between Mr. Ahmad Khan (hereinafter the "Landlord") and Ms. Sara Ali (hereinafter the "Tenant") for the premises located at House 12, Canal Town, Peshawar.

1. TERM
The lease shall commence on 1st January 2026 and continue for a period of eleven (11) months.

2. RENT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of Rs. 45,000 on or before the 5th day of each month. The Landlord may increase the rent at any time at his sole discretion.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of Rs. 90,000, which is non-refundable and shall be forfeited in full for any breach, however minor.

4. ACCESS
The Landlord may enter the premises at any time without prior notice for inspection or any other purpose.

5. TERMINATION
The Landlord may terminate this tenancy at any time without cause on seven (7) days notice. The Tenant must give ninety (90) days written notice before vacating the premises.

6. DEFAULT AND EVICTION
If the Tenant fails to pay rent within 48 hours of the due date, the Landlord may evict the Tenant immediately without any court process. The Tenant shall pay a penalty of 5% per day on all overdue amounts.

7. REPAIRS
The Tenant shall be solely responsible for all repairs, maintenance and damage to the premises, including structural repairs.

8. WAIVER
The Tenant waives all rights and claims against the Landlord arising out of this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement.
